# Clustering Lab

Interactive seminar tool for **LUBS5302M Foundations of Machine Learning in Business** (Leeds University Business School).

Students can step through k-means and hierarchical clustering on the lecture's customer data, Spanish bank data, example shapes, or points they draw themselves.

Everything runs in the browser; nothing is uploaded or stored on a server.

Spanish bank data: Serrano-Cinca (1997), analysed in Mar-Molinero and Serrano-Cinca (2001), *European Journal of Finance* 7(2), 165–183.
